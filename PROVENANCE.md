## Rules for future contributions

Before adding third-party content, record:

- upstream project and URL;
- upstream version, tag or commit when practical;
- applicable license/terms;
- whether material was copied, translated, adapted or only used as conceptual reference;
- files or directories affected.

Do not describe third-party or translated material as wholly original Ventura work. Do not assume that an inspiration project's license automatically applies to newly authored Ventura material, or vice versa.
